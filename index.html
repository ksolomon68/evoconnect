Vendor Readiness Checklist

Know Exactly What You Need

Our interactive checklist breaks the path to federal contracting into three clear tracks so you always know your next step.

  • ✓Get a free DUNS/UEI number (SAM.gov prerequisite)
  • ✓Register on SAM.gov (free — allow 2–3 weeks)
  • ✓Identify your NAICS codes (we do this automatically)
  • ✓Open a dedicated business bank account
  • ✓Write a one-page capability statement
  • ✓Apply for HUBZone, WOSB, or 8(a) if you qualify
  • ✓Search SAM.gov for active subcontracting opportunities
