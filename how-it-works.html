Your Path to a Government Contract

How WorkForce Connect Works

From registration to your first federal contract — four steps, no fees, no guesswork.

1
Step 1 — Takes 5 Minutes

Create Your Free Worker Profile

Register your trade, years of experience, certifications, and location. There are no fees — ever. Your profile is the foundation for everything that comes next.

  • Enter your name, contact info, and location
  • Select your primary trade from 13 eligible categories
  • Choose your travel willingness (local, regional, or national)
  • List any existing certifications (CDL, OSHA, BICSI, etc.)
2
Step 2 — Instant & Automatic

Get Matched to Federal NAICS Codes

NAICS (North American Industry Classification System) codes are the identifiers federal agencies use to search for and award contracts. The moment you register, WorkForce Connect automatically maps your trade to every relevant NAICS code — no research required.

  • Your trade is matched to 2–4 federal contract codes instantly
  • NAICS codes appear on your registration confirmation
  • Use these codes when registering on SAM.gov (Step 3)
  • Multiple trades? Register with multiple NAICS codes
3
Step 3 — Work at Your Own Pace

Complete the Vendor Readiness Checklist

Our interactive checklist walks you through three tracks: getting on SAM.gov (the federal contractor database), registering with your state, and preparing your subcontracting documents. Each track has clear milestones so you always know your next action.

  • Track A: SAM.gov federal registration (free, ~2–3 weeks)
  • Track B: State certifications (HUBZone, WOSB, 8(a), SDVOSB)
  • Track C: Sub-ready documents (capability statement, bank account, business entity)

View the Full Checklist →

4
Step 4 — Ongoing

Get Connected to Prime Contractors

Prime contractors working on federal and state projects search our database for workers with your trade, certifications, and location. When your profile matches what they need, they contact you directly through the platform — no job boards, no middlemen.

  • Prime contractors filter by trade, location, and travel willingness
  • You receive connection requests in your dashboard
  • High-demand trades (fiber, broadband, CDL) get matched fastest
  • Completing more of the vendor checklist increases your match priority

Ready to Get Started?

Registration is free and takes less than 5 minutes. Your NAICS match results are delivered instantly upon completion.
